## 🎓 UNDERSTANDING THE THREE-STAGE SYSTEM

### **Stage 0: Master Concept Ontology** (Reference Library)
**Purpose:** "What does this concept mean across all texts?"  
**Format:** Encyclopedia-style concept entries  
**Use Case:** Defining terms, understanding theoretical foundations  
**Example:** "What is the 'inciting incident' according to McKee vs. Field vs. Truby?"

### **Stage 1: Deep Analytical Decomposition** (Text-by-Text Analysis)
**Purpose:** "How does this specific author's system work?"  
**Format:** Detailed methodology breakdowns per author  
**Use Case:** Deep dive into individual theorist's complete approach  
**Example:** "How does Truby's 22-step structure work in detail?"

### **Stage 2: Master Synthesis** (THIS DOCUMENT SYSTEM)
**Purpose:** "How do I USE these concepts? How do I combine them?"  
**Format:** Workflows, guides, matrices, tools  
**Use Case:** Actual writing work—solving problems, building projects  
**Example:** "I'm writing a feature comedy—what's my complete workflow?"

**HOW THEY WORK TOGETHER:**
- **Stage 0** = Dictionary (look up definitions)
- **Stage 1** = Textbooks (study individual systems)
- **Stage 2** = Workshop manual (apply and integrate)
