⸻

Each Pass = a self-contained module executed under the Production Control Loop (PCL):

[Input Draft vN]
   ↓
[Pass Module Pn]
   ↓
[Validation & Telemetry]
   ↓
[Governance Ledger]
   ↓

2.10 Pass 7 — Delivery Pass (#p7-delivery)

Objective: Produce submission-ready deliverables and governance records.
