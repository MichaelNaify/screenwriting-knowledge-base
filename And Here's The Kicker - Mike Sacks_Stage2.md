F.1 Ontology of the Comic (#being-as-bent-structure)

Thesis: Comedy assumes reality is patterned but pliable. Writers bend ordinary rules—etiquette, institutions, language—just enough to show their arbitrariness.
	•	Entities: Characters are vectors of status and need, not lists of traits.
	•	World: A rule-field where the “game” of a scene gives local physics.
	•	Becoming: Identity is performed contradiction (confidence + incompetence) stabilized as persona.

F.2 Epistemology of Humor (#how-we-know-with-laughter)

Claim: Laughter is recognition—evidence that a reframe “fits.” The anthology implies pragmatic epistemology:
	•	Empirical: Bits are true insofar as they work across rooms/editors (#pragmatic-validity).
	•	Falsifiability: Repeated failure (after timing calibration) refutes the premise (#bomb-evidence).
	•	Inference: The Gap (expectation vs result) is a micro-experiment: surprise → meaning repair (#prediction-error → #meaning-repair).

Tags: #enacted-knowledge #recognition-signal #joke-as-experiment

F.3 Ethics of Targeting (#moral-architecture-of-satire)

Consensus rule: punch up. Targets should have agency/power; otherwise jokes slide to bullying.
	•	Cost Accounting: Weigh harm-per-laugh; mis-educating the audience is a net negative (#audience-stewardship).
	•	Self-first: Aim inward before outward; signals fairness.
	•	Revision Ethics: Retire or revise material when contexts shift (#post-bit-ethic).

Tags: #punching-up #proportionality #consent-boundaries

F.4 Aesthetics of Fit (#beauty-as-coherence-of-bit)

	•	Clarity: Setup legible on contact (#setup-promise).
	•	Economy: No beat without change (#beat-economy).
	•	Inevitability: The turn surprises yet retrofits the setup (#turn-and-reveal).
	•	Button: Funniest last word (#last-word-win).

Tags: #comic-economy #inevitable-surprise #button-design

⸻

F.5 Time, Causality, Chance (#narrative-physics-of-funny)
	•	Causality: Even absurdism runs on local law; pure randomness decays laughs.
	•	Chance: Coincidence may ignite, never resolve, a bit (#earned-punch).

Tags: #game-integrity #temporal-craft

Tags: #liberty-through-limits #format-discipline
