What was once mystical inspiration becomes a procedural science. This shift defines Field as the Galileo of narrative practice — translating observation into measurement.

Field’s legacy is conceptual clarity joined to human empathy: a vision of structure as service to feeling. Even in the era of nonlinear streaming and interactive worlds, his grammar of expectation, conflict, and resolution endures as the basic algorithm of audience engagement. The Fieldian screenplay is more than blueprint; it is the mind’s rehearsal for change, a bridge between private intuition and collective understanding.
