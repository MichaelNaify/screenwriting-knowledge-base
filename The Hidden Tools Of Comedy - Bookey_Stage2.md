#1 Comedy as Cognitive Ethics

Stage 2 reframes Kaplan’s “ordinary person struggling against insurmountable odds” as a cognitive-ethical algorithm.
Comedy, in this synthesis, performs three simultaneous operations:
	1.	Cognitive Calibration – Re-tunes expectation and perception.
	2.	Affective Homeostasis – Converts anxiety to laughter, restoring balance.
	3.	Ethical Rehearsal – Allows audiences to test empathy in safe failure.

Kaplan’s formula becomes a universal learning loop:

Error → Recognition → Renewal.

Every comic beat trains the mind to integrate contradiction.  Comedy is thus a neuro-moral pedagogy: a pattern-recognition system for compassion.
