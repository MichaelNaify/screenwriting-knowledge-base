PHASE 2 — PRIMARY CONCEPTS (Level 1 Tagging)

#YES-AND (Foundational Ethic)

Affirm partner’s reality then add information. Eliminates #blocking and trains co-creation reflex. Parallel to Johnstone’s “accept offers,” but Besser targets comedic heightening under game logic.

#GAME-OF-THE-SCENE (Core Engine)

Pattern of behavior or idea becoming comic driver. Process: identify unusual thing → ask why → heighten & explore. Comparable to Truby’s “designing principle.”

#SUPPORT-MOVES (Ensemble Scaffolding)

Non-initiating actions that sustain momentum without usurping focus (tag-outs, walk-ons, edits). Demonstrates trust and collective rhythm → #group-mind.

#HEIGHTENING (Comic Amplification)

Escalating pattern within game logic while maintaining truth. Predictive reward loop for audience.

#BASE-REALITY (Context Anchor)

Grounded world before the unusual element appears; gives comic contrast.

#LISTENING (Attentional Discipline)

Active reception of partner’s offers. Trains responsive awareness (#embodied-cognition).

#COMMITMENT (Performative Integrity)

Total belief in invented circumstance; authenticity magnifies absurdity. Ethical respect for scene & partner.

⸻



ADDED SECTION — ANTI-PATTERNS & SAFETY PROTOCOLS

Anti-Patterns
	•	#blocking: Denying offers or facts.
	•	#wimping: Vague offers that avoid decision.
	•	#pimping: Forcing others into awkward jokes for a laugh.
	•	#gagging: Choosing humor over truth or scene integrity.

Safety & Consent
	•	Establish boundaries before shows or classes.
	•	Use “edit on discomfort” protocol (sweep or call scene).
	•	Debrief after sensitive content.
	•	Treat ensemble trust as ethical infrastructure.
