# §2 APPLIED SCENE DESIGN & PERFORMANCE METRICS (#scene-design)

Stage 4 Chunk C transforms Kaplan–Bookey's cognitive ethics into scene-level engineering protocols. It defines how each line, gesture, and beat can be measured, graphed, and refined without extinguishing spontaneity. The scene becomes a controlled experiment in empathy and timing.

## 2.1 Scene Blueprint (#scene-blueprint)

Every comic scene is built around one truth revelation. Writers document its architecture in four rows:

| Component | Purpose | Diagnostic Variable | Ideal Metric |
|:--|:--|:--|:--|
| Setup | Establish illusion | Expectation Level | Baseline audience belief score ≈ 0.9 |
| Contradiction | Break pattern | Pain Amplitude | 4–6 / 10 |
| Recognition | Reveal truth | Cognitive Bridge Latency | 0.3–1 s |
| Empathy Reset | Re-bond audience | Empathy Index | 0.6–0.8 |

This chart parallels Field's three-act proportion (Setup / Conflict / Resolution) but functions at micro-temporal scale. #beat-architecture #truth-engineering

## 2.2 Beat Economy (#timing-protocol)

Kaplan's dictum "Comedy is truth plus pain" converts to a timing equation:

```text
Beat Interval = (f × ΔT_truth) / ΔT_pain
```

Where `f` = frequency of audience prediction error. Lower ratios (< 1.0) signal rushed beats; higher ratios (> 3.0) signal drag. Directors and editors graph beat intervals per scene to balance cognitive load. #timing-science #editing-rhythm

## 2.3 Empathy Heat-Curve (#affective-mapping)

The Empathy Heat-Curve plots viewer connection over time:

```text
0.0 ───────────────────────────── 1.0 (Empathy)
│    ╭───╮        ╭──╮
│   ╭╯   ╰╮╭────╮╭╯  ╰╮
└───┴─────┴┴────┴┴────┴───▶ Time
```

Each laugh momentarily drops empathy (through distance) and each truth beat restores it. Scripts that finish above 0.6 average are classified as warm comedy; below 0.5 = ironic comedy. Educators teach tone control via heat-curve visualization. #affective-analytics #tone-governance

## 2.4 Performance Metrics Dashboard (#actor-analytics)

Actors log quantitative and qualitative data per rehearsal:

| Variable | Instrument | Range | Purpose |
|:--|:--|:--|:--|
| Beat Latency | Stopwatch or DAW timeline | 1–3 s | Micro-timing control |
| Eye Fixation Duration | Camera tracking | 0.2–0.6 s | Authenticity index |
| Audience Reaction Delay | Video log | 0.3–1 s | Response validation |

Metrics feed Stage 5's AI-based feedback loops. #embodied-data #actor-dashboard
