## 6 · The Rewrite Matrix (Structural Diagnostics)

|:--|:--|:--|:--|

## 7 · Scene Physics — The Boot Camp Equation

Writers use this equation as a quick diagnostic tool: if a scene leaves characters and audience unchanged, cut or rewrite.
