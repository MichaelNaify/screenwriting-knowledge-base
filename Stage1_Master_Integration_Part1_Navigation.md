## 1.2 MASTER SOURCE TEXT INVENTORY

### Complete Collection (24 Texts)

#### **DRAMA & SCREENWRITING FUNDAMENTALS**

**1. Story - Robert McKee** (1997)
- **Domain**: Dramatic theory, scene craft, story design
- **Unique Contribution**: Philosophical foundation, controlling idea, subtext mastery
- **Best For**: Scene craft, deep dramatic principles, story values
- **Difficulty**: Advanced - requires multiple readings
- **Integration**: Pairs with Truby (architecture) and Seger (practice)

**2. Anatomy of a Story - John Truby** (2007)
- **Domain**: Story architecture, character systems, theme integration
- **Unique Contribution**: 22 building blocks, character web, moral premise
- **Best For**: Character-driven narratives, complex architecture, theme integration
- **Difficulty**: Advanced - comprehensive system mastery required
- **Integration**: Pairs with McKee (philosophy) and Field (structure)

**3. Screenplay: The Foundations of Screenwriting - Syd Field** (1979/2005)
- **Domain**: Structure, paradigm, three-act framework
- **Unique Contribution**: Paradigm teaching tool, plot points, clear act divisions
- **Best For**: Beginners, structural foundation, teaching
- **Difficulty**: Beginner-friendly - very accessible
- **Integration**: Foundation for all other structural approaches

**4. Making a Good Script Great - Linda Seger** (1987/2010)
- **Domain**: Script analysis, problem-solving, revision
- **Unique Contribution**: Systematic troubleshooting, character spine, practical fixes
- **Best For**: Diagnosing problems, revision, character voice
- **Difficulty**: Intermediate - assumes basic knowledge
- **Integration**: Complements any methodology (practical problem-solving)

**5. Save the Cat! - Blake Snyder** (2005)
- **Domain**: Genre patterns, beat sheet, commercial screenwriting
- **Unique Contribution**: 15-beat structure, Save the Cat moment, genre types
- **Best For**: Commercial features, genre films, accessibility
- **Difficulty**: Beginner-friendly - very accessible
- **Integration**: Pairs with Field (structure) and Truby (depth)

#### **ADVANCED SCREENWRITING & REWRITING**

**6. Advanced Screenwriting - Linda Seger** (2003)
- **Domain**: Advanced techniques, complex structure, mastery
- **Unique Contribution**: Non-linear narratives, multiple protagonists, sophistication
- **Best For**: Complex structures, artistic films, advanced technique
- **Difficulty**: Advanced - requires strong fundamentals
- **Integration**: Builds on Seger's earlier work

**7. Rewrite - Paul Chitlik** (2011)
- **Domain**: Revision, development, professional rewriting
- **Unique Contribution**: Systematic rewrite methodology, note incorporation, professional polish
- **Best For**: Revision, professional development, systematic improvement
- **Difficulty**: Intermediate to professional
- **Integration**: Pairs with Seger (problems) and professional practice texts

#### **ADAPTATION THEORY & PRACTICE**

**8. A Theory of Adaptation - Linda Hutcheon** (2006/2012)
- **Domain**: Adaptation theory, transmedia, fidelity
- **Unique Contribution**: Academic theory, fidelity critique, cultural translation
- **Best For**: Adaptation theory, justifying choices, transmedia
- **Difficulty**: Advanced - academic/theoretical
- **Integration**: Pairs with Owen (practice)

**9. The Art of Screen Adaptation - Alistair Owen** (2017)
- **Domain**: Adaptation craft, research, practical technique
- **Unique Contribution**: Comprehensive practical guide, research methodology, source analysis
- **Best For**: Any adaptation project, research, practical craft
- **Difficulty**: All levels - very comprehensive
- **Integration**: Pairs with Hutcheon (theory) and Seger (technique)

## 1.8 INTEGRATION PHILOSOPHY

### Core Principles

**1. Synthesis Over Allegiance**
- No single framework is complete
- Integration creates power
- Recognize strengths and limitations of each

**2. Context Determines Application**
- Different problems require different frameworks
- Genre, format, and goals affect choice
- Flexibility over rigid adherence

**3. Progressive Development**
- Master basics before integration
- Build foundations first
- Synthesize over time

**4. Practical Over Theoretical**
- Apply immediately
- Write, don't just study
- Learn through doing

**5. Personal Methodology**
- Develop unique synthesis
- Honor creative process
- Build custom approach
