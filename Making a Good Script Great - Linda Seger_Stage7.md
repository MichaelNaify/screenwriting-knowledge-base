# §3 SCENE DYNAMICS & EMOTIONAL ARCHITECTURE #scene-dynamics #emotional-architecture

## 3.1 Scene as System Node #system-node #narrative-physics  

A scene is the **smallest self-sustaining dramatic organism**.  
Inputs = character intention + contextual pressure.  
Process = conflict → decision → value shift.  
Output = new story state.

When a scene fails, entropy enters the system; Stage 7 treats this as measurable data loss.  
Each rewrite restores equilibrium by realigning intention and obstacle.

## 3.2 Seger’s Three Energy Laws #scene-energy  

1. **Conservation of Tension** – Every beat must transfer emotional energy, not dissipate it.  
2. **Acceleration Principle** – Each succeeding scene must raise kinetic or moral velocity.  
3. **Thermodynamic Resolution** – Release occurs only when the audience’s emotional energy equals the protagonist’s revelation.

Seger transforms McKee’s “gap” and Field’s “progressive complications” into energetic equations:  

\[
E_{scene}=ΔV_{emotion}×R_{stakes}
\]

_(Stage 7 synthesis of Field/McKee constructs; house metric.)_

Where ΔV = change in value state and R = risk magnitude.
