# §1 MATHEMATICAL MODELS & PERFORMANCE INDICES (#math-models)

⸻

## 1.1 Purpose (#purpose)

⸻

## 1.2 Model Overview (#overview)

Rewrite performance is expressed as:

$REI = f(E, \Theta, S\sigma, D\rho, \kappa)$

Where each term measures energy (E), theme (Θ), structure (Sσ), dialogue (Dρ), and iteration (κ).
The Rewrite Efficiency Index (REI) is the core governance signal of Stage 6 (#efficiency-index).

⸻

## 1.3 Base Equation (#base-equation)

$REI = \dfrac{E \times \Theta \times \kappa}{(S\sigma + \varepsilon) \times D\rho}$, with $\varepsilon = 10^{-4}$ for numerical stability.

Constraints: $E, \Theta, D\rho \in [0,1]$; $S\sigma \le 0.1$; $\kappa \in [1,10]$.
Optimization → $REI \rightarrow 1$ when emotional strength, moral coherence, and discipline converge (#optimization-target).

⸻

## 1.4 Supporting Indices (#indices)

Index | Formula | Purpose
---|---|---
Scene Variance Index (SVI) | $SVI = E_{t+1} - E_t$ | Scene-to-scene energy stability
Theme Alignment Coefficient (TAC) | $TAC = \Theta / S\sigma$ | Moral coherence vs structure
Dialogue Economy (DE) | $DE = 1 - D\rho$ | Subtext efficiency
Cadence Consistency (CC) | $CC = \kappa / \max(\kappa)$ | Iteration discipline
Composite Quality (CQ) | $CQ = (REI + TAC + DE + CC)/4$ | Overall creative health

These indices feed Stage 6 dashboards (#composite-metrics).

⸻

## 1.5 Predictive Performance Model (#predictive)

$REI_{n+1} = \alpha E + \beta \Theta - \gamma S\sigma - \delta D\rho + \varepsilon' \kappa + \eta$

Coefficients ($\alpha$–$\varepsilon'$) calibrated through least-squares regression of ≥ 100 rewrite cycles.
Target forecast variance ≤ 5 %. Predicts final evaluation before cycle completion (#forecast-engine).

⸻

## 1.6 Creative Volatility Metric (#volatility)

$V = \sqrt{\frac{1}{N}\sum_{i=1}^{N}(REI_i - \bar{REI})^2}$

Interpretation → $V < 0.05$ = stable cycle; $0.05 \le V \le 0.1$ = moderate fluctuation; $V > 0.1$ = instability (#stability-audit).
