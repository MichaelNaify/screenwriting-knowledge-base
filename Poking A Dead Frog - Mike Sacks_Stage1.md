EXECUTIVE SUMMARY

1. Document Overview

Poking a Dead Frog (2014) is the sequel-expansion to Mike Sacks’s And Here’s the Kicker (2009). It continues his deep-dive oral-history project on contemporary comedy writing, assembling over 40 long-form interviews, annotated essays, and process documents with major figures from film, television, print humor, and emerging digital formats.

The book is not a “how-to manual.” Instead, it functions as a living archive of professional practice, combining practitioner testimony, informal theory, and the tacit epistemology of comedy creation. Each conversation discloses how comedy is conceived, written, edited, pitched, and culturally situated.

Sacks operates as both journalist and ethnographer: he elicits fine-grain detail about routines, habits, insecurities, and failures that conventional manuals omit. The text thus bridges oral history ↔ craft manual ↔ cultural study, positioning itself within the lineage of The Paris Review Interviews and American Masters, yet focused entirely on comedy as art and labor.
